Your Kholleta real-account balance is denominated in USD. Mobile-money settlement is in UGX using the rate shown on each offer.

How peer-to-peer works

Choose a BUY USD or SELL USD offer using MTN or Airtel. The correct payer sends UGX to the other trader. The mobile-money recipient confirms receipt, then the matching USD amount moves between the two Supabase balances.

Never share your mobile-money PIN or one-time password. Do not mark a payment as sent unless you completed it.

Create a P2P offer

BUY USD: the offer owner sends UGX and receives USD from the accepting trader. SELL USD: the offer owner provides USD and receives UGX from the accepting trader. All registered profiles, including admin profiles, can publish offers. Settlement transfers USD between the two Supabase accounts after mobile-money receipt is confirmed.
